# Light Wars

## About the Game
Light Wars is an intense, color-matching space shooter where you battle through various waves of enemies using the power of the Chromatic Spectrum. You must dynamically match your laser color to your enemies, strategize your ammo by crafting orbs, and master the "Inverted Mode" to defeat powerful bosses!

## Mechanics
- **Normal Mode:** Shoot enemies with the same color laser as their shields to destroy them.
- **Inverted Mode:** Invert the light spectrum! This flips the rules and is the only way to defeat certain invincible bosses.
- **Ammo & Crafting:** Manage your RGB and CMY ammo. Defeated enemies drop color fragments which you can use to craft new ammo orbs.

## Controls
- **WASD / Arrows:** Move your ship
- **Left Mouse Button (LMB):** Fire Laser
- **Right Mouse Button (RMB) / E:** Toggle Inverted Mode
- **Number Keys (1-7):** Switch Laser Colors (Red, Green, Blue, Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, White)
- **Space / Enter:** Advance dialogue and story
